# Polyclinic No. 5 Appointment Booking

A university team project about making it easier for patients
to book appointments at Polyclinic No. 5 in Astana.

## Problem
Patients faced busy phone lines and long queues. The project
also addressed the risk of double bookings.

## My contribution
I worked on testing and analysis. One scenario we considered
was a patient selecting a time slot that was already unavailable.
The proposed flow lets the patient return to the list and choose
another time.
